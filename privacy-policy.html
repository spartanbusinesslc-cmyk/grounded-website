Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who We Are

EARTHED is a UK-based natural skincare brand operating at shopearthed.co. We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy. We are committed to protecting your personal data and being transparent about how we use it. You can contact us at support@shopearthed.co.

What Data We Collect

When you place an order or interact with our website, we may collect the following information:

  • Name and email address
  • Delivery address
  • Payment information (processed securely by Stripe — we never store card details)
  • Order history and preferences
  • Your email address, if you sign up to our mailing list through the pop-up or footer form
  • IP address and basic browser data
  • Website usage data (pages visited, device type and approximate location), collected through Google Analytics

How We Use Your Data

We use your data only for the following purposes:

  • Processing and fulfilling your orders
  • Sending order confirmation and dispatch emails
  • Responding to customer enquiries
  • Sending marketing emails, if you have signed up to our mailing list or bought from us and have not opted out
  • Understanding how our website is used, so we can improve it and our products
  • Complying with legal obligations

We will never sell your personal data to third parties.

Third Parties

We share your data with the following trusted third parties only where necessary to run our shop, fulfil your order and communicate with you:

  • Stripe — for secure payment processing. Stripe is PCI-DSS compliant. View their privacy policy at stripe.com/gb/privacy.
  • Royal Mail — for order fulfilment and delivery.
  • Klaviyo — for sending marketing and order-related emails. View their privacy policy at klaviyo.com/legal/privacy-notice.
  • Zapier — to pass contact details from our payment system to our email service.
  • Google Analytics — to measure how our website is used. View Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Vercel — our website hosting provider, which also provides basic site performance statistics.

Some of these providers are based outside the UK, including in the United States. Where this happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards required by UK data protection law, such as approved transfer agreements.

Cookies

Cookies and similar technologies are small files or browser storage entries that websites use to work and to understand how they are used. We use the following:

  • Essential: your shopping cart is saved in your browser so it is still there when you come back, and we remember if you have closed our sign-up pop-up. If you arrive through a referral link, we store the referral code in a cookie for 30 days so the referral can be credited.
  • Payments: Stripe, our payment provider, may set cookies or similar technologies to process payments securely and prevent fraud.
  • Analytics: we use Google Analytics, which sets cookies to help us understand how many people visit our website, which pages they view and how they found us. We use this only to improve our website and do not use it for advertising.

We do not use advertising cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can stop Google Analytics from collecting data about you by installing the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of the website, such as the shopping cart, from working.

Email Marketing

If you sign up to our mailing list, or buy from us and have not opted out, we may send you emails about our products and offers. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of every email, or by contacting us. We keep your email address on our mailing list until you unsubscribe.

Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict how we process your data
  • Lodge a complaint with the ICO at ico.org.uk

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@shopearthed.co.

Data Retention

We retain order data for up to 6 years in line with UK tax and accounting requirements. Mailing list details are kept until you unsubscribe. You may request deletion of your data at any time, subject to these legal obligations.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your data:

  • Instagram: @shopearthed
