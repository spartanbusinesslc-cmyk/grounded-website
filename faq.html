Every EARTHED product is made from a small number of natural, ancestral ingredients — things like grass-fed tallow, raw cow's milk, beeswax, olive oil, coconut oil and shea butter. No synthetic fillers, no parabens, no seed oils. Just ingredients that have nourished skin for centuries.
If you've noticed names like limonene, citral or linalool in our ingredients list, these are not synthetic additives — they are naturally occurring compounds found within the essential oils we use, such as lemon, lime and lavender. UK and EU cosmetic regulations require these to be declared by name on the label when present above a certain concentration, even when they occur naturally. They are simply part of what makes a natural essential oil smell the way it does. If you have a known sensitivity to any of these compounds, we recommend opting for our Plain (unscented) varieties, which contain none of them.

It's the kind of ingredient our ancestors used long before synthetic skincare existed.
