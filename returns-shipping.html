Returns, Refunds & Shipping

Last updated: October 2026

Shipping

All orders are dispatched via Royal Mail. Next-day dispatch guaranteed: we guarantee to dispatch every order the next working day after it is placed. Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding UK bank holidays.

  • UK: Standard delivery 2–3 working days · Free on orders over £20 · Otherwise £3.99
  • Europe: Standard delivery 5–10 working days · Free on orders over £75 · Otherwise £15
  • Rest of World: Standard delivery 7–15 working days · Free on orders over £75 · Otherwise £20

Once your order has been dispatched you will receive a confirmation email. The guarantee applies to when we dispatch your order. Delivery times are estimates, so please allow the full delivery window before contacting us about a late order.

Returns & Refunds

60-day satisfaction guarantee. We want every customer to be completely happy with their order. If you are unhappy or dissatisfied with your product for any reason, including if it arrives damaged or faulty, contact us within 60 days of receiving it and we will provide a replacement or a refund.

To make a claim:

  • Contact us within 60 days of receiving your order
  • Send us photos of the product and tell us what the problem is, or why you are not satisfied
  • Reach us via Instagram @shopearthed or email support@shopearthed.co

The guarantee covers all of our products, including damaged items and subscription orders. Once we have reviewed your request, we will process your refund or send a replacement within 5 working days. Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

If any items are sent back to us, return postage must be paid by the buyer.

Your legal right to cancel. Separately from our guarantee, under the Consumer Contracts Regulations you have the right to cancel an online order within 14 days of receiving your goods, without giving a reason. To cancel, tell us by email or Instagram within that time. Please return the items to us within 14 days of telling us, and we will refund you within 14 days of receiving them (or of receiving proof that you have sent them back). This right does not apply to sealed products that have been opened after delivery and cannot be returned for hygiene reasons. Those items are still covered by our 60-day satisfaction guarantee above.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time by contacting us before your next renewal date. Please reach out via Instagram or email and we will cancel your subscription with no questions asked. Our 60-day satisfaction guarantee also applies to subscription deliveries.

Subscriptions renew automatically at the frequency you chose at checkout (monthly or every 3 months) until you cancel. You will be charged the price shown at checkout for each delivery, plus delivery where it applies. To stop future deliveries, contact us before your next renewal date.
